# Artwork direction

Built-in image generation was used for all six original section assets. This document records the creative briefs, not a verbatim export of the generation prompts.

Shared brief: detailed Japanese-inspired pixel art matching the homepage's warm lanterns, deep navy atmosphere, wood architecture and crisp illustrative detail. No headline, logo, UI labels or prominent embedded text. Environments must be distinct from the homepage and each other.

- Games: wide Japanese arcade entrance beside a quiet waterside street. Warm wooden interior, arcade cabinets, stools, hanging lanterns, blossoms and vending machine; inviting night light. Daylight variant opens the scene to clear sky, fresh green planting, natural wood and bright reflections.
- Videos: portrait composition of a cozy recording/editing studio. Desk, microphone boom, headphones, camera and multiple editing monitors; Japanese rooftops and distant pagoda outside. Daylight variant uses sunlit surfaces and bright mountain/rooftop scenery while retaining a recognizable studio composition.
- News: panoramic cybersecurity monitoring room facing a Japanese waterfront city. Network racks, technical monitors, desk and chair; warm interior against a blue night skyline. Daylight variant uses a bright harbor, daylight through broad windows, natural wood and greenery.

Dark assets were generated as new environments. Light assets were generated as image variants of their corresponding dark environment. Exports preserve proportions and use WebP compression; no CSS recoloring or brightness filtering is used.
